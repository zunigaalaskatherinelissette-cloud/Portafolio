02 / ABOUT ME · GET TO KNOW ME

Who I am.✳

Katherine Lissette Zuniga Alas · Student · Creative thinker · Future medical professional

I’m Katherine Lissette Zuniga Alas, a high school student who describes herself as curious, responsible, creative, and eager to learn. I enjoy working with others, organizing ideas, and paying attention to details. I try to approach each challenge with patience, empathy, and the willingness to improve.

My interest in technology began with curiosity about how websites and digital tools are created and how they can make everyday tasks easier. Through school projects, I discovered that I enjoy combining creativity with logic: planning an idea, designing an experience, learning how the code works, and improving the result. Building projects has helped me turn that curiosity into practical experience with web development, databases, and digital tools.

What motivates me is the possibility of using what I learn to make a positive difference. I want to continue growing in technology while also preparing for a future in medicine, a field that connects knowledge with care and service. I believe that staying curious, working with purpose, and treating people with empathy will help me keep moving toward those goals.
